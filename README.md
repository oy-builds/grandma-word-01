# One Word From Grandma — "smartypants"

Entry for the make.fun bounty *One Word From Grandma*: exactly one word — the
word grandma would text about AI.

She tried the chatbot. It answered everything at once, in full sentences,
without being asked twice. Her one-word verdict, texted to the family group
chat: **smartypants**.

`main.ts` (Node, program/v1) writes that single word to `/output/entry.txt`.
